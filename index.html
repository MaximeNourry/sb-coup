SOVIET BUREAU OF CRYPTIC OCCURRENCES & UNKNOWN PHENOMENONS

An alternative cold war tabletop RPG inspired by the SCP foundation. You are an Agent of the Soviet Bureau of Cryptic Occurrences and Unknown Phenomenons. Your job is to protect the motherland from darkness. As to your pension... It is theoretical.

01 — THE GAME

A GAME ABOUT STUDYING THAT WHICH DOES NOT WANT TO BE UNDERSTOOD

In SB-COUP, the Administrator (Your usual game master) writes a Scene and runs it.
Everyone else play as Agents: Soldiers and scientists sent into madness by a bureaucratic organization that could not care less for their fates.
Scenes can swing from political spy intrigue to surreal horror. Let your imagination loose. Only ████████is the limit !

Note: A Session 0 is strongly recommended. SB-COUP, through its horror aspects, can be triggering for certain people. It is therefore essential to set boundaries together as to what might be described by The Administrator and what themes might be explored.

02 — SETTING

AN ALTERNATE SOVIET UNION WHERE REALITY IS AS STABLE AS THE SPIRIT.

When They told me that my talents were wasted in the Kremlin. I did not understand.
When they told me my work on DNA was only child’s play. I did not understand.
When they told me I was but pretending to be a god. I did not understand.
But I do know. I have seen gods.
And they hate us.

DR KATARINA MIESCHER — RESEARCHER
SB-COUP takes place in an alternate Soviet Union filled with unforgiving paranormal entities. In order to protect the population from those Anomalies, secret groups dedicated to their study, containment and neutralization were founded. One of them is The Soviet Bureau of Cryptic Occurrences and Unknown Phenomenons: The SB-COUP

This project began in the summer of 2023. It started as a few notes inspired by art pieces such as Stalker, Control, House of Leaves, and of course the SCP Foundation.
Two years later, the game is now fully playable.
